## General overview:

This is a game where the grass has been tired of waiting for you to touch it ,and it makes you go outside . It mainly focuses on exploration and going outside and absurdity.

## Plot:

The plot is you follow around the protagonist exploring the weird ,surreal  and esoteric things shown by the grass which has grown tired of not being touched.You start in your home while a strange grass block asks you to come out and then you travel to hell , moon , all the normal stuff xD .

## Controls:

Use WASD for movement and space bar for dialogue boxes.

## Techincal :
I used kaplay framework in javascript which is specifically made for 2d gamedev and was quite a help !
